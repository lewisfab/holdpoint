Holdpoint Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Holdpoint is a ballistics calculator published by LewisFab. This policy explains what the app does with information.

What we collect

Nothing. Holdpoint does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it does not request internet access.

Data stored on your device

Your settings and saved setups (scope, ammunition, distances and weather values you enter) are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Children

Holdpoint is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
